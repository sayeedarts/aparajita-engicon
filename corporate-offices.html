Industry overview

Engineering Modern Work Environments

Corporate offices require dependable building infrastructure that supports employee comfort, operational efficiency and uninterrupted business activities. Aparajita Engicon provides multidisciplinary engineering and contracting solutions for corporate offices, business headquarters and modern workplace developments.

Our capabilities cover plumbing and public health engineering, fire protection, HVAC, electrical systems, water treatment and associated civil works. By coordinating these disciplines, we help create functional, comfortable and maintainable office environments.
