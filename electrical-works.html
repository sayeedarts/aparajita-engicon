Electrical Engineering & Installation
Overview

Electrical infrastructure supports the safe and dependable operation of modern buildings. Aparajita Engicon undertakes internal electrification, external electrification, electrical panels and control-related installation works for residential, commercial, hospitality, institutional and industrial developments.

Our approach focuses on coordinated installation, dependable distribution, equipment integration, testing and compliance with approved electrical designs and applicable safety requirements.

Our electrical services

Scope of services

Our Electrical Services

Internal electrification covers electrical distribution and installation within a building, supporting lighting, equipment, power outlets and other designated electrical loads.

Scope of work

  • Internal electrical wiring and distribution
  • Lighting and power circuits
  • Electrical conduit and cable routing
  • Cable tray and cable management installation
  • Distribution board installation and connections
  • Earthing connections, where specified
  • Electrical equipment connection coordination
  • Inspection, testing and commissioning support
Our approach

We coordinate electrical routing with architectural layouts, ceiling systems, plumbing, HVAC and other building services.

External electrification provides electrical distribution infrastructure across outdoor areas and designated site facilities.

Scope of work

  • External electrical distribution networks
  • Underground and surface cable routing
  • External cable trench coordination
  • Outdoor lighting infrastructure
  • Feeder and distribution connections
  • External electrical equipment connections
  • Earthing and lightning-protection interfaces, where specified
  • Testing and commissioning support
Our approach

External electrical works are coordinated with site development, underground utilities, civil works and approved electrical layouts.

Electrical panels and control systems distribute electrical power and provide the switching, protection and control interfaces required by building equipment.

Scope of work

  • Electrical panel installation
  • LT distribution panel connections
  • Motor Control Centre (MCC) panel connections
  • Equipment power connections
  • Control panel installation and interface coordination
  • Cable termination and identification
  • Integration with designated mechanical equipment
  • Electrical inspection and testing support
Our approach

Panel installation and equipment connections are coordinated with approved electrical schematics, load requirements, protection arrangements and equipment specifications.
