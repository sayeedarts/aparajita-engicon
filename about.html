Our story

Three decades of engineering, built on dependable delivery

Established in 1990, Aparajita Engicon Pvt. Ltd. has grown into a multidisciplinary engineering and contracting company, trusted for plumbing, fire fighting, civil, electrical and HVAC works across hospitality, residential and commercial environments.

Our teams combine qualified engineers and trained site professionals to execute complex requirements with technical depth, disciplined planning and clear communication, from the first estimate to handover and beyond, with structured AMC support on selected projects.
