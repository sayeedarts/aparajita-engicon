Swimming Pool Engineering
Overview

A swimming pool requires carefully coordinated hydraulic, filtration and circulation systems to maintain water quality and reliable operation. Aparajita Engicon provides swimming pool plant engineering and installation solutions for hospitality, residential, recreational and institutional facilities.

Our approach integrates pool circulation, filtration equipment, pumping systems and associated plumbing infrastructure to support efficient water treatment and dependable pool operations.

Our swimming pool services

Scope of services

Our Swimming Pool Services

We provide swimming pool plant solutions designed to circulate, filter and treat pool water in accordance with the requirements of each facility.

Scope of work

  • Swimming pool filtration plant installation
  • Circulation and filtration pump systems
  • Pool water filtration equipment
  • Pool plumbing and circulation networks
  • Plant-room piping and equipment connections
  • Water treatment equipment integration
  • Equipment installation and coordination
  • Testing and commissioning support
  • Plant modernization and upgrade support
  • Maintenance support as agreed under contract
Our approach

We coordinate plant equipment, hydraulic arrangements, pipework and operational access to support effective circulation, water treatment and maintainability.
