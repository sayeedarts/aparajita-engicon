Welcome to Aparajita Engicon

Engineering excellence built on three decades of dependable delivery

Established in 1990, Aparajita Engicon Pvt. Ltd. is an engineering and contracting company delivering plumbing, fire fighting, civil, electrical and HVAC works across Delhi NCR and PAN India. For over three decades, our multidisciplinary teams have executed projects across hospitality, residential and commercial environments with technical depth, disciplined execution and dependable support after handover.
