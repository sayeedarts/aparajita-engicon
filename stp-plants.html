STP Plant Engineering
Overview

Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) are essential for treating domestic wastewater generated by residential, commercial, hospitality and institutional developments. A properly designed and installed STP supports wastewater treatment, responsible discharge and potential treated-water reuse in accordance with applicable requirements.

Aparajita Engicon provides STP-related engineering and installation services, coordinating mechanical equipment, process piping and associated infrastructure in accordance with approved project designs.

Our STP services

Scope of services

Our STP Services

We undertake installation and coordination of sewage treatment equipment and associated mechanical infrastructure for building and development projects.

Scope of work

  • STP equipment installation coordination
  • Treatment tank and equipment interface coordination
  • Process piping and interconnecting networks
  • Pumps, valves and associated fittings
  • Blower and aeration equipment connections
  • Sludge handling equipment connections, where specified
  • Treated-water outlet and distribution interfaces
  • Electrical and control interface coordination
  • Testing and commissioning support

Reliable wastewater collection and transfer infrastructure supports the effective operation of a sewage treatment system.

Scope of work

  • Wastewater collection piping
  • Sewage transfer pump connections
  • Pumping system and valve arrangements
  • Inlet and outlet piping coordination
  • Inspection and access arrangements
  • Integration with building sewerage networks

We coordinate the piping and mechanical interfaces required for the movement of wastewater and treated water through the treatment process.

Scope of work

  • Interconnecting process piping
  • Equipment inlet and outlet connections
  • Pumps, valves and fittings
  • Air and water line connections, where specified
  • Pipe supports and installation coordination
  • Equipment interface checks

Treated water may be directed toward approved reuse applications, depending on treatment quality and project requirements.

Scope of work

  • Treated-water outlet connections
  • Storage and transfer arrangements
  • Reuse distribution piping
  • Connections to designated reuse systems
  • Integration with landscaping or flushing systems, where specified
  • Testing and commissioning support

We support the final installation checks and commissioning activities associated with STP mechanical systems and their interfaces.

Scope of work

  • Mechanical installation inspection
  • Equipment connection checks
  • Piping and leakage checks
  • Operational testing support
  • Interface coordination with electrical and control systems
  • Maintenance support as agreed under contract
